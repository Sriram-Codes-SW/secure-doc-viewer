# Review records

These files are the records of how the book was reviewed and checked. They are not part of the book.

- `final-review/`: the final verification: independent reviews of the finished book (senior engineer, architect, technical lead, accessibility), their rechecks, a QA sweep, the proofreaders' edit logs, the author's decisions on their queries, the layout rationale, accessibility tool results with versions, a screen-reader test script, and the final technical review.
- `reviews/`: one QA review per chapter, and a status file.
- `CHARTER.md`: the rules the writing team worked under. `glossary_src.tsv`: the source of the glossary. `tradeoffs-evidence.md`: the evidence behind Chapter 37.

All of the reviewers, writers and proofreaders were AI agents working under the direction of the project owner, and the records say so. Two kinds of working material were deliberately left out of this repository: the reconstructed project dossier (which quoted the project owner's messages) and the agents' running progress logs. Local file paths and agent identifiers in the remaining records were replaced with placeholders. Some records mention working files that are not included here.
