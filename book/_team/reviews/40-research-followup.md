## Notes for the chapter fixes
- Item 1 and item 3a interact: even with Spring's graceful phase raised, Fargate kills the container at `stopTimeout` (maximum 120 s). The two numbers to state together: Spring 30 s default, Fargate 30 s default and 120 s maximum.
- Item 2 changes Solution 41.3 and Listing 41.2: `s3:ListBucket` is a requirement for the service role, not "if the app lists".
- Item 4 and the HSTS row in Table 40.1: replace "the ALB does not add HSTS" with "the ALB does not add HSTS unless you configure a listener attribute; this design keeps HSTS in nginx".
- Item 7 needs a concrete pair of numbers in Chapter 40 and 41: ALB idle timeout N, nginx `keepalive_timeout` greater than N, and `proxy_read_timeout 300s` at least as long as the longest upload.
- Method limits: items 3a (default 30 s), 3b (`configure-action` under the new prefix), 10a and 10d rely on documentation summaries or inference and are flagged in the rows. No repository or chapter files were edited.
